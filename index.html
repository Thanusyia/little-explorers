Welcome
How to use this flipbook

For children

  • Tap next and previous to turn the pages.
  • Click the flashcards to flip them.
  • Choose answers in quiz time.
  • Play the matching game at the end.

For adults

  • Use the printable companion for tracing and colouring.
  • Read each word in three languages.
  • Repeat aloud and let the child point to the correct answer.
  • Use 10–15 minutes per section.
